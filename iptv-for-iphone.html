IPTV for iPhone & iPad: Setup Guide

General guidance for using a compatible IPTV player on iPhone and iPad.

IPTV on iOS and iPadOS

iPhone and iPad users can access IPTV through compatible IPTV player applications available for their device.

What You Need

  • iPhone or iPad
  • Stable internet
  • Compatible IPTV player
  • Active subscription details

General Setup

  1. Connect the device to the internet.
  2. Install a compatible IPTV player from the device's app store.
  3. Open the player.
  4. Enter the subscription details provided by the service.
  5. Allow the player to load the available content.
  6. Test playback.
