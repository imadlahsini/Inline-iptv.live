What Is IPTV EPG?

Learn what an IPTV EPG is, how electronic program guides display schedules and why EPG data can sometimes be incomplete.

EPG stands for Electronic Program Guide. It is schedule information that helps a player display program names, timings and other metadata.

What an EPG Displays

Depending on the available data, an EPG may show channel names, program titles, descriptions, start times and end times.

Why EPG Data Can Be Incomplete

Schedules can be missing or misaligned when source metadata is delayed, a channel identifier changes, time-zone information differs or the player has not refreshed its data.

EPG Sync Issues

Refreshing the playlist or guide data, checking the device time and reopening the player can resolve some display issues.
