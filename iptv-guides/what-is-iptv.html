What Is IPTV?

Learn what IPTV is, how IPTV delivers television over the internet and what devices and connection requirements are involved.

IPTV stands for Internet Protocol Television. It delivers television and video content over an internet connection instead of traditional terrestrial, satellite or cable broadcasting.

How IPTV Is Different From Traditional TV

Traditional television commonly relies on broadcast, satellite or cable delivery. IPTV sends requested video data through internet protocols, allowing compatible players to present live or on-demand content.

What You Need to Use IPTV

You generally need an active subscription, a stable internet connection, a compatible player and an internet-connected device.

Common IPTV Devices

Common options include Smart TVs, Fire TV Stick, Android devices, iPhone, iPad and Apple TV when a compatible player is available. See the compatible devices page.

IPTV Players and IPTV Services

An IPTV service provides access details, while a player application uses those details to organize and play available streams.

Live TV and On-Demand Content

Depending on the service and subscription, IPTV may present live television, sports and on-demand entertainment.

Internet Connection Requirements

Connection quality depends on video quality, network congestion, Wi-Fi conditions and simultaneous usage. See our internet speed guide.
