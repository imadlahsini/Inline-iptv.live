IPTV for Android Phones, Tablets & Android TV

Use a compatible IPTV player on Android devices with an active subscription.

IPTV on Android

Android devices can use compatible IPTV player applications to connect to an active IPTV subscription.

What You Need

  • Android phone, tablet or Android TV
  • Stable internet
  • Compatible player
  • Active subscription credentials

General Setup

  1. Connect the device to the internet.
  2. Install a compatible IPTV player.
  3. Open the player.
  4. Enter the subscription details provided by the service.
  5. Load the available content.
  6. Test playback.

Troubleshooting

  • Update the IPTV player.
  • Check login details.
  • Restart the device.
  • Switch between Wi-Fi and wired connection where possible.
  • Check available storage.
