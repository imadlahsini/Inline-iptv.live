IPTV vs Cable TV

Compare IPTV and cable TV by delivery method, device flexibility, internet requirements and viewing experience.

IPTV and cable TV use different delivery methods and hardware. The practical choice depends on your connection, devices and viewing preferences.

TopicIPTVCable TV
DeliveryInternet protocol deliveryCable network delivery
Internet requiredYesNot necessarily for the television signal
HardwareCompatible device and playerCable equipment and provider hardware
PortabilityCan be used on compatible internet-connected devicesOften tied to installed service and equipment
On-demand supportDepends on the service and playerDepends on the cable provider
InstallationApp and subscription setupProvider installation may be required
Viewing devicesSupported Smart TV, streaming and mobile devicesTelevision and provider-supported equipment

Choose Based on Your Needs

Consider your internet connection, preferred devices, available services and local requirements. See the device guide and subscription information.
