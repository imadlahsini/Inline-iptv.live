What Internet Speed Do You Need for IPTV?

Learn what internet speed helps support smooth IPTV streaming in SD, HD and 4K and how to improve connection stability.

Internet speed needs vary with video quality, compression, network congestion, Wi-Fi quality and simultaneous device usage. The following conservative guidance can help with planning:

QualityApproximate connection
SDApproximately 5 Mbps
HDApproximately 10 Mbps
Full HDApproximately 15 Mbps
4KApproximately 25 Mbps or more

Actual requirements vary based on compression, network congestion, Wi-Fi quality and simultaneous device usage.

Improve Connection Stability

Place the device within good Wi-Fi range, reduce competing downloads, restart network equipment when appropriate and use Ethernet when possible.

Check Your Setup

If playback remains inconsistent, review the buffering troubleshooting guide and visit the Help Center.
