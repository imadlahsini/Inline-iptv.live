How Does IPTV Work?

Understand how IPTV works, from internet delivery and IPTV players to live channels, on-demand content and supported devices.

IPTV combines an internet-delivered video service with a player application on a compatible device.

Server

The service makes available video streams and related information through servers. The exact infrastructure varies by provider.

Internet Delivery

Video data travels across an internet connection to the viewing device. Network quality affects how consistently data arrives.

Player Application

An IPTV player reads the subscription login or playlist information and organizes available streams for viewing.

Device

The player runs on a compatible Smart TV, Firestick, Android device, iPhone, iPad, Apple TV or another supported platform.

Stream Playback

When you select content, the player requests the stream and decodes it for display. Buffering may occur when delivery cannot keep up with playback.
